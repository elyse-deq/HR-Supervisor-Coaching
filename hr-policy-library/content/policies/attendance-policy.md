## Purpose
This policy sets expectations for reliable attendance and on-time arrival so that teams can plan coverage.

## Reporting an absence
Employees must notify their supervisor as soon as possible, and no later than one hour before the start of the shift, when they cannot attend. Supervisors record the absence in the timekeeping system the same day.

## Supervisor responsibilities
Supervisors apply this policy consistently, hold a documented conversation when a pattern of unplanned absence or lateness appears, and involve HR before any formal step is taken. Supervisors do not make disciplinary decisions on their own.

## Patterns and follow-up
A pattern is three or more unplanned absences or late arrivals within a rolling 30-day period. When a pattern appears, the supervisor schedules a private conversation, listens for barriers, restates expectations, and documents the discussion using the approved template.

## Absences that may involve leave or accommodation
If an employee mentions a medical condition, family situation, or need for accommodation, the supervisor stops the attendance conversation and contacts HR. Supervisors do not ask for medical details.
