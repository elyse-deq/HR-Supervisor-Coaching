## First response
Meet with each person separately, listen without taking sides, and clarify what happened and what each person needs. Keep notes factual.

## Resolving routine disagreements
For routine workplace disagreements, help both people agree on how they will work together, and check back within one week.

## Situations that go to HR immediately
Any report involving harassment, discrimination, threats, retaliation, or physical safety goes to HR immediately. The supervisor does not investigate.
