## Principles
Address performance early, focus on observable behavior and specific examples, and keep the conversation private and respectful.

## Structure of the conversation
Open by stating the purpose. Describe the specific behavior and its impact. Ask the employee for their perspective. Agree on clear expectations and a follow-up date. Document the conversation the same day.

## When to involve HR
Involve HR before issuing any written warning, performance improvement plan, or formal corrective action. Supervisors coach and document; HR reviews all formal steps.
