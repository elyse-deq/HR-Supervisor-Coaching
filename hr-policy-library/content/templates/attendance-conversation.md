## Opening
"I wanted to talk privately about attendance. I have noticed some recent absences and I would like to understand how I can support you."

## Key points
State the specific dates. Restate the reporting expectation. Ask about barriers. Agree on next steps.

## Documentation format
Date of conversation, dates discussed, expectations restated, employee response, agreed next steps, follow-up date.
