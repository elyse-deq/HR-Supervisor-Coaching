## Purpose
Correct the behavior quickly, explain why it matters, and confirm the safe way to do the task.

## Prepare
- Address any immediate hazard first, before the conversation.
- Know the specific procedure or rule involved.
- Note what you observed, not what you assume.

## Opening language
"I saw [specific behavior]. I want to talk about it because [safety reason]."

## Key questions
- Walk me through how you approached the task.
- What made the safe method hard to follow?
- What would make it easier to do this the right way every time?

## What to avoid
- Delaying the conversation until it is convenient.
- Treating a near miss as unimportant.
- Assigning blame before you understand what happened.

## When to involve HR
Whether HR must be involved is decided by the escalation rules, not by this guide. Check them before advising. Common prompts to check:
- There was an injury or a serious near miss.
- The behavior repeats after coaching.
- The employee reports pressure to skip safety steps.

## Follow up
Confirm the safe method, check it on the next shift, and document the conversation.
