## Before you talk
- I know the specific policy or expectation involved.
- I have facts, dates, and examples, not impressions.
- I have chosen a private setting and enough time.
- I know what outcome I am aiming for.
- I have checked the escalation rules for this type of situation.
- I have reviewed any documented history.
