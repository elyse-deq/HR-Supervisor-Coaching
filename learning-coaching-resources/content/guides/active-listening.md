## Skills
- Reflect back what you heard in your own words.
- Ask one open question at a time.
- Notice what is not being said, and invite it gently.
- Resist filling silence.

## Practice exercise
Have a colleague describe a work problem for two minutes. Summarize it without adding advice, then ask what they need from you.
