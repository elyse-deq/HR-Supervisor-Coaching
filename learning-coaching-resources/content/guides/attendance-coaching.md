## Purpose
Understand what is behind an employee's pattern of arriving late, leaving early, or missing shifts, restate the expectation, and agree on a next step.

## Prepare
- Review the attendance policy and the facts (dates, arrival times, and missed shifts) before you talk.
- Choose a private space and enough time.
- Separate the pattern from assumptions about the reason.

## Opening language
"I have noticed [pattern]. I want to understand what is going on and make sure you have what you need."

## Key questions
- What has been getting in the way?
- Is there anything about the schedule or workload we should look at together?
- What would help you meet the expectation going forward?

## What to avoid
- Guessing at reasons, especially health or family circumstances.
- Promising outcomes or consequences.
- Comparing the employee to coworkers.

## When to involve HR
Whether HR must be involved is decided by the escalation rules, not by this guide. Check them before advising. Common prompts to check:
- The employee mentions a medical condition, leave, or accommodation.
- The employee raises harassment, discrimination, or retaliation.
- The pattern continues after a documented conversation.

## Follow up
Summarize the agreement, share it with the person, and document the conversation.
