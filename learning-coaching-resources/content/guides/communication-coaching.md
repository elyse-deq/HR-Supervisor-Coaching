## Purpose
Make the impact of the communication style clear and agree on what good looks like.

## Prepare
- Pick one or two concrete examples.
- Describe the behavior and its effect, not the person's character.
- Consider whether the expectation was ever stated.

## Opening language
"I noticed [specific example]. Here is how it affected [work or team]. I would like us to talk about what works better."

## Key questions
- How did you see that situation?
- What was the intent?
- What would you do differently next time?

## What to avoid
- Labels like "rude" or "difficult."
- Making it about personality.
- Ignoring cultural, language, or channel differences that may play a role.

## When to involve HR
Whether HR must be involved is decided by the escalation rules, not by this guide. Check them before advising. Common prompts to check:
- The communication involved discriminatory or threatening language.
- The employee says it was a response to mistreatment.
- The issue persists after coaching.

## Follow up
Agree on one or two observable changes and check in on them.
