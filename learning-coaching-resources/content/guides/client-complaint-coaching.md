## Purpose
Share the feedback fairly, hear the employee's account, and agree on improvement and any follow-up with the client.

## Prepare
- Gather the facts and the exact feedback received.
- Separate what the client said from your own interpretation.
- Decide what you can and cannot share about the source of the complaint.

## Opening language
"We received feedback about [specific situation]. I would like to hear what happened from your side."

## Key questions
- What was going on when that happened?
- Is there anything we could have done to support you?
- How would you handle it next time?

## What to avoid
- Assuming the complaint is accurate or inaccurate before listening.
- Sharing client details beyond what is needed.
- Promising a specific outcome for the client.

## When to involve HR
Whether HR must be involved is decided by the escalation rules, not by this guide. Check them before advising. Common prompts to check:
- The complaint alleges harassment, discrimination, or a safety violation.
- The complaint involves a legal or contractual concern.
- The same complaint recurs.

## Follow up
Agree on actions, close the loop with the client through the appropriate channel, and document.
