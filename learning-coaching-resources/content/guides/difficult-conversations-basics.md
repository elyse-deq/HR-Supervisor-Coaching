## The four steps for a difficult conversation
1. Prepare. Every difficult conversation goes better when you know the facts, the expectation, and your goal.
2. Open. State the purpose and the specific observation.
3. Listen. Ask open questions and let the person respond fully.
4. Agree. Confirm next steps, who does what, and when you will follow up.

## Practice prompts
- Say the observation in one sentence without adjectives.
- Rewrite an accusation as a question.
- Practice a pause before responding to a strong reaction.

## Common traps
Rushing to solutions, softening the message until it disappears, and guessing at motives.
