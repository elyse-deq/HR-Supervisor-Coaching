## Purpose
Understand each perspective, keep the focus on work behavior, and agree on how the team will work together.

## Prepare
- Talk to each person separately first.
- Identify the specific behaviors and their effect on the work.
- Stay neutral and do not take sides.

## Opening language
"I want to understand what has been happening between you and [role or team]. I am here to listen first."

## Key questions
- What happened, from your point of view?
- How has it affected your work?
- What outcome would you like to see?

## What to avoid
- Judging who is right before hearing everyone.
- Sharing one person's comments with the other.
- Dismissing the concern as personality.

## When to involve HR
Whether HR must be involved is decided by the escalation rules, not by this guide. Check them before advising. Common prompts to check:
- Any conduct involves harassment, discrimination, threats, or violence.
- Someone fears retaliation.
- The conflict continues after you have coached both people.

## Follow up
Agree on specific behaviors, check in within a set time, and document each conversation.
