## Purpose
Describe the gap clearly for an employee who is underperforming, understand barriers, and agree on specific support and next steps.

## Prepare
- Define the expectation and gather specific examples of the gap.
- Check whether the person had the training, tools, and clarity they needed.
- Decide what support you can offer.

## Opening language
"I want to talk about [specific work outcome]. Here is what I have seen and what I need to see."

## Key questions
- How do you see it?
- What is getting in the way?
- What support would help you meet the expectation?

## What to avoid
- Vague feedback such as "attitude" or "not a team player."
- Bundling many issues into one conversation.
- Deciding consequences yourself. Those involve HR.

## When to involve HR
Whether HR must be involved is decided by the escalation rules, not by this guide. Check them before advising. Common prompts to check:
- You are considering any formal action.
- The employee raises a medical, leave, or accommodation issue.
- The employee alleges unfair treatment.

## Follow up
Set a date to review progress, write down the agreed steps, and document the conversation.
